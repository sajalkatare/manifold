# leave_policy.md  (dept=all, access=internal)

## Sick Leave
All employees are entitled to 5 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two days.

## Parental Leave
Employees with at least one year of service are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave per child, requested at least 30 days in advance.

## Earned Leave
Earned leave accrues at 18 days per year after the first year. Up to 30 days may be carried forward.
